Andrews Appiah scored a superb opening goal as FK Auda beat Riga FC 2-0 in the Latvian Cup final at Skonto Stadium on Friday, securing the trophy for the second successive year.

The 18-year-old Ghanaian put Auda ahead in the 29th minute, finding the far top corner with an excellent strike from the edge of the penalty area.

Appiah's goal proved decisive. Youba Traoré doubled Auda's lead in the 59th minute after a corner, and the holders saw out the match to claim their third Latvian Cup title, following triumphs in 2022 and 2025.

It was not Appiah's first important contribution to Auda's cup run. He also scored in the semi-final against BFC Daugavpils, when Auda fought back from 2-0 down to draw 2-2 before advancing on penalties.

Just six days before the final, SkyrScout published Scout Pilgrim's scouting report on Appiah, highlighting his explosive acceleration, finishing ability with both feet and powerful shooting from distance. His cup-final strike was another example of why the young attacker has made such an impression since arriving in Latvia.

The one concern for Appiah was that he had to leave the pitch with an injury early in the second half. The extent of the injury remains unclear.
