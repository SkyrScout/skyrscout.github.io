Colombian centre-forward Kevin Quejada has signed for Al-Zawraa SC, leaving Venezuelan side Estudiantes de Mérida for the Iraq Stars League.

Quejada was featured by SkyrScout in April 2026 during an impressive spell with Estudiantes. At the time of our report, the 187 cm striker had recorded 9 goals and 1 assist in 10 Liga FUTVE matches.

Scout Pilgrim highlighted his physical strength, intelligent movement and positioning inside the box, as well as his ability to finish with either foot and his head. Before moving to Venezuela, Quejada had also helped Cúcuta Deportivo secure promotion to Colombia's Primera A in 2025.

The move continues a well-travelled career that has already taken Quejada through Colombia, Brazil, Argentina, Portugal and Venezuela, with Iraq now becoming his latest destination.
