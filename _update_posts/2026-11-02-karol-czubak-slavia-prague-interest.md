Karol Czubak is reportedly emerging as **Slavia Prague's primary striker target** ahead of the winter transfer window.

Polish journalist Karol Tyniec reports that the Motor Lublin centre-forward is currently the main name on Slavia's list as the Czech club considers options for its attack.

Kanał Sportowy, citing Tyniec's report on X, says Czubak is ahead of other names under consideration, including Rafał Adamski and Szymon Włodarczyk. TransferFeed has since carried the same information.

Adamski is a particularly notable name on that list. Slavia already made a concrete move for the Legia Warszawa striker in the summer, with Meczyki reporting that a **€2 million offer was rejected**. The Czech club then continued to monitor him and sent representatives to watch him live against Radomiak Radom, where Adamski scored in a 5-0 win.

SkyrScout has also profiled Adamski in detail, meaning two of the strikers now mentioned on Slavia's reported shortlist already have full scouting coverage on the site.

At this stage, there is **no reported bid or direct contact with Motor Lublin over Czubak**, so his part of the story remains one of reported transfer interest rather than negotiations.

The link comes at a logical point in Czubak's career.

The 26-year-old scored 18 league goals for Motor in the 2025/26 Ekstraklasa season and has followed that with six goals in 13 league appearances so far this term. He also recently told Kanał Sportowy that he still wants to test himself at a higher level and would like the opportunity to play abroad.

Czubak has also appeared in the Czech discussion around Slavia's search for a striker. A recent Médium.cz article named him as one of three possible replacements for Tomáš Chorý and praised his physical presence, work rate and link play.

That article should not be treated as independent confirmation of Slavia's interest, however. It is primarily the author's assessment of players who could fit the club, rather than a report that negotiations are taking place.

Czubak's contract situation also gives Motor some protection. He joined the club in the summer of 2025 on a two-year deal with an option to extend for a further two years, meaning he is not simply approaching the end of his agreement without the club having additional control.

The latest report follows previous transfer interest around Czubak. In August, he was linked with Levante, while Motor signalled at the time that they were not interested in entertaining offers for their striker.

SkyrScout first profiled Czubak during his time with Arka Gdynia in December 2024, highlighting his finishing, movement inside the penalty area and consistent goalscoring output.

For now, Slavia's reported interest has not progressed to a bid or negotiations.

But if Czubak really is first on their striker list, this is one to keep watching.
