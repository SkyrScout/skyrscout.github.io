Sander Alamaa was the hero again as **Flora Tallinn U19 beat Chelsea U19 3-2 at Cobham and advanced 5-4 on aggregate in the UEFA Youth League**.

The 18-year-old winger scored twice and assisted Flora's third goal in the second leg, completing an extraordinary two-match performance against one of Europe's best-known academies.

Alamaa opened the scoring after 17 minutes before Chelsea turned the match around through Mathis Eboue and Ibrahim Rabbaj.

Flora responded in the second half. Alamaa supplied the assist for Marten Kukkonen to make it 2-2 in the 54th minute, before stepping up from the penalty spot in the 78th minute to score the winner.

That gave Alamaa **two goals and one assist in the return leg**.

And it came after an equally decisive first meeting in Tallinn.

In the first leg on 28 October, Alamaa scored both Flora goals in a 2-2 draw. His first came after just four minutes, cutting inside from the left and curling a finish beyond the Chelsea goalkeeper. He later converted a second-half penalty to level the match.

Across the two legs, his numbers against Chelsea read:

**4 goals  
1 assist  
5 goal contributions**

Before the first match, Chelsea coach Harry Hudson had spoken positively about Flora's youth development and said his staff had studied the Estonian side carefully.

They had certainly seen Alamaa coming.

Stopping him proved rather more difficult.

SkyrScout profiled Alamaa earlier this year, highlighting his acceleration, one-v-one ability, direct running and surprisingly strong aerial game for a winger.

Against Chelsea, he delivered the kind of two-match performance that tends to get noticed.
