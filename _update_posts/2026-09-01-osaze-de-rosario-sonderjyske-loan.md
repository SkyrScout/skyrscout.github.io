Osaze De Rosario is reportedly close to joining Sønderjyske Fodbold on loan from Seattle Sounders.

Sounder at Heart, citing Niko Moreno, reports that Seattle are finalizing a deal that would send the 25-year-old centre-forward to the Danish Superliga, likely through the end of the 2026/27 season. TransferFeed also reports that the move is subject to a medical and final details.

The move would give De Rosario a new opportunity for regular senior football after his role in Seattle became more limited during the 2026 season.

There is also a neat SkyrScout connection. When we profiled De Rosario in April 2025 at Tacoma Defiance, we highlighted his 189 cm frame, athleticism, pace and physical presence, and specifically noted that his profile could translate well to the Nordic leagues.

Sønderjyske may now provide exactly that test.
