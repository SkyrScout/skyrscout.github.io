Mamadou Sylla has been named Tonybet Virslīga Player of the Month for July after scoring four goals in three appearances across two clubs.

The 20-year-old began the month on loan at BFC Daugavpils, scoring a hat-trick in a victory over Liepāja. After returning to parent club RFS, he added another goal in two league appearances.

Sylla has now scored six goals in 21 Virslīga appearances this season. His performances during the first half of the campaign earned him a recall from RFS, where he has already made an impact following his return.
