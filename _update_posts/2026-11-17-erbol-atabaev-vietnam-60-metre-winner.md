Erbol Atabaev produced a remarkable late show for Kyrgyzstan, providing the assist for the equaliser before scoring an extraordinary winner from his own half in a 2-1 victory over Vietnam.

Vietnam had led from the 19th minute through Nguyễn Đình Bắc and appeared set to hold on until Kyrgyzstan turned the match around in the closing stages.

In the 86th minute, Atabaev delivered the corner that captain Valerii Kichin headed in at the far post to make it 1-1.

Just two minutes later came the decisive moment.

After Vietnam lost possession, Atabaev spotted goalkeeper Trần Trung Kiên well off his line and launched an audacious effort from around 60 metres, inside his own half. The ball sailed over the goalkeeper and into the net to complete the comeback.

The goal gave Kyrgyzstan a 2-1 victory in the final match of the Casper Cup 2026 and secured the tournament title.

It was another spectacular moment from the 25-year-old attacking midfielder, whose long-range shooting, vision and set-piece quality have already featured prominently in SkyrScout's assessment of him.
