Raki Aouani has been **recalled to the Tunisia senior national-team squad** for the upcoming AFCON 2027 qualifiers against Libya.

The Riga FC midfielder is included in the 27-man squad for the two November fixtures, giving him another opportunity to make his senior international debut.

Aouani has been called into the Tunisia senior setup before. He was part of the squad in May 2024 for World Cup qualifiers against Equatorial Guinea and Namibia, but did not make an appearance.

That makes the November window notable for a different reason: **his first senior cap is still in sight**.

The call-up comes after a strong period at club level. Aouani has established himself with Riga FC and has also attracted reported interest from abroad, with La Presse recently linking both Charleroi and FC Zürich with the 21-year-old.

DailySports described Aouani as a first-time senior call-up, but that is not accurate. The more interesting story is that he is back in the Tunisia squad and could now finally make his senior international debut.

SkyrScout profiled Aouani earlier this year, highlighting his explosive acceleration, excellent dribbling, progressive ball-carrying and ability to threaten from either wing.
