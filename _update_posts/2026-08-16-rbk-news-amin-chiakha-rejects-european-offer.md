Amin Chiakha has reportedly rejected an official offer from an unnamed European club competing in the Champions League this season because he wants to remain at Rosenborg.

Algerian outlet Maghreb-Foot says it spoke directly with Chiakha's father and representative, Nabil Chiakha, about the striker's future.

According to Nabil, Amin has a clear long-term ambition to eventually play in England or Germany. However, he recently received a formal offer from what his father describes as a respected European club participating in this season's Champions League and decided not to accept it after considering the proposal.

His father says the decision was not an isolated one. Chiakha has reportedly rejected other approaches as well because he currently wants to stay at Rosenborg, where he has settled well and feels valued.

### A significant development for Rosenborg

The situation is particularly interesting because Chiakha is still owned by F.C. Copenhagen.

The 20-year-old joined Rosenborg on loan in January, with the agreement running through the remainder of 2026. FCK's official announcement also confirms that Rosenborg have a purchase option included in the loan agreement.

The size of that option has been reported differently by several outlets, while FCK have not publicly disclosed the amount. It should therefore not be treated as confirmed.

Any permanent stay at Lerkendal would still require Rosenborg to resolve the contractual situation with FCK, but Chiakha's reported desire to remain gives RBK an encouraging position if they decide to pursue a permanent transfer.

### Ten goals in 18 official appearances

Chiakha has increasingly established himself as one of Rosenborg's most important attacking players.

Rosenborg's official statistics show that he has scored **10 goals in 18 competitive appearances** for the club: nine goals in 17 Eliteserien matches and one goal in his only Norwegian Cup appearance.

RBK's own player statistics also credit him with two assists in the Eliteserien.

His form has accelerated significantly during the summer. Most recently, Chiakha scored the winning goal five minutes into stoppage time as Rosenborg defeated reigning Norwegian champions Viking at Lerkendal on August 14.

That was his ninth league goal of the season.

The performances naturally increase the likelihood of interest from elsewhere, but if his father's account is accurate, the most significant part of the story is not simply that European clubs are watching him.

It is that Chiakha is reportedly prepared to turn down immediate opportunities elsewhere, including an offer from a Champions League participant, because he currently wants to continue at Rosenborg.

That does not mean a permanent transfer is agreed or guaranteed.

But with a purchase option already included in the loan agreement, 10 goals in 18 official appearances and the player himself reportedly wanting to stay, Rosenborg appear to have several important pieces in place if they decide to try to keep him beyond 2026.
