Mohamed El Amine Ramdaoui's proposed move to Baltika Kaliningrad has fallen through, with Russian champions Zenit St Petersburg now reportedly interested in the Paradou AC striker.

According to Africafoot, citing DZfoot, Paradou and Baltika had agreed a €1.2 million transfer fee, payable in two instalments, plus a 30% sell-on clause. However, Ramdaoui decided not to complete the move after spending three days in Russia, with the player reportedly unconvinced by life in the isolated Kaliningrad exclave.

DZfoot now reports that Zenit St Petersburg are monitoring Ramdaoui as another potential option in Russia. The 20-year-old scored 10 league goals for Paradou AC during the 2025/26 season, finishing as the second-highest scorer in the Algerian top flight.
