Wendell Gabriel scored a hat-trick as FCI Levadia cruised to a 5-0 victory over Nõmme United in the Estonian Premium Liiga.

The Brazilian forward struck his first goal in first-half stoppage time to make it 3-0, then added another just two minutes after the restart.

Wendell completed his hat-trick in the 87th minute, finishing off a dominant league victory for Levadia.

Fellow SkyrScout player Maksimilian Skvortsov also contributed, providing the assist for Rasmus Peetson's 2-0 goal in the 30th minute.

The victory keeps Levadia at the top of the Premium Liiga table as Wendell continues an impressive season in Estonia.
