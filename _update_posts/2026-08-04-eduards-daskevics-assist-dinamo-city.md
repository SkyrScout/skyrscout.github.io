Eduards Daskevics delivered another decisive European contribution as FK Auda beat Dinamo City 1–0 in the first leg of the UEFA Conference League third qualifying round.

The Latvian international set up Barthelemy Diedhiou for the only goal of the match in the 68th minute, giving Auda a narrow advantage ahead of the return leg in Albania.

Daskevics has now made a direct contribution in three consecutive European matches. He scored in the first leg against FCSB before providing assists in the return match and against Dinamo City.
