Mohamed El Amine Ramdaoui has signed for USM Alger on a five-year contract running until summer 2030, according to Africafoot.

The 2005-born striker was previously set to join Baltika Kaliningrad, with Paradou AC reportedly agreeing a €1.5 million deal with the Russian club, but the transfer was never completed. USM Alger subsequently revived their interest and reached an agreement with both Paradou and the player.

The move keeps Ramdaoui in Algeria after a summer that also saw interest from abroad, bringing an end to the uncertainty surrounding his immediate future.
