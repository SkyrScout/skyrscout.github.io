Raki Aouani is reportedly attracting fresh European interest, with **Charleroi and FC Zürich both scouting the Riga FC attacker**.

According to AfricaFoot, scouts representing the Belgian and Swiss clubs have watched Aouani's last three matches in the Latvian league and UEFA Conference League.

There is no confirmed bid, negotiation or direct club contact at this stage, but repeated live scouting from two named clubs represents a concrete step beyond general transfer speculation.

The timing is notable because Aouani continues to produce eye-catching performances for Riga.

In Riga FC's 2-1 win over SK Super Nova on 18 October, the club's own post-match statistics showed Aouani leading the match in **five separate categories**:

**14 successful dribbles from 17 attempts (82%)  
5 successful crosses from 11 attempts (45%)  
14 successful progressive passes from 15 attempts (93%)  
335 metres of sprinting distance  
32.89 km/h maximum speed**

The combination of volume and efficiency stands out particularly in possession. Aouani completed 14 dribbles while also completing 14 progressive passes, and Riga's data ranked him as the match leader in both categories.

AfricaFoot reports that Charleroi and FC Zürich are monitoring the 22-year-old Tunisian with a view towards a possible future move. For now, however, the situation remains at the **live scouting / reported interest** stage.
