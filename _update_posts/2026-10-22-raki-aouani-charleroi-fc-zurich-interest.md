Raki Aouani is reportedly attracting fresh European interest, with Charleroi and FC Zürich keeping a close eye on the Riga FC attacker.

According to AfricaFoot, scouts from both clubs have watched Aouani's last three appearances in the Latvian league and UEFA Conference League.

The repeated live scouting suggests that both clubs are actively assessing the 22-year-old Tunisian ahead of a possible move. There is no confirmed official offer at this stage, and no report of negotiations or direct club contact.

Aouani has recorded six goals and five assists in 31 appearances across all competitions for Riga FC this season and is under contract with the Latvian club until June 2028.

The latest interest follows previous reports during the summer transfer window linking Aouani with Hungarian side Győr FC.
