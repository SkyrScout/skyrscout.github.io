Jonathan Karlsson has taken another step forward in Sweden, completing a permanent move to Västerås SK before scoring his first Allsvenskan goal shortly afterwards.

VSK confirmed on 12 October that the 22-year-old full-back had joined permanently from Hammarby after spending time with the club on loan. Karlsson signed a deal running through the 2029 season.

Eleven days later, he reached another milestone.

Karlsson scored his first Allsvenskan goal against Halmstads BK on 23 October, finishing a well-worked move to put Västerås 1-0 ahead just before half-time. Halmstad eventually came back to win the match 2-1.

The permanent move follows a strong period at VSK for the quick and attack-minded full-back, who can operate on either side.

Karlsson also holds a small piece of SkyrScout history. A Scouty Pippen classic from the very beginning of the project, he was the subject of the **first scouting video ever published on the SkyrScout YouTube channel**. That video also became the channel's **first to pass 1,000 views**, back when SkyrScout was still in its earliest stages.

From a Superettan scouting video to a permanent Allsvenskan move and a first goal in Sweden's top flight: a nice full-circle moment for one of the originals.
