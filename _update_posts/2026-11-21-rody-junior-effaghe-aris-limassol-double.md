Rody Junior Effaghe scored twice after coming on at half-time as Aris Limassol came from behind to beat Nea Salamis 3-1 on Saturday. The 22-year-old Gabonese striker now has nine goals and one assist in ten Cypriot top-flight appearances this season.

With Aris trailing 1-0 at the break, Effaghe replaced Dennis Gaustad for the second half. Oriyomi Lebi equalised in the 51st minute before Effaghe rose to meet a corner from Veljko Nikolić, powering a header home to put Aris ahead in the 79th minute. He added his second in the fourth minute of stoppage time, finishing a counterattack with his right foot to seal the victory.

His nine league goals already equal his total from 28 appearances in the 2025/26 campaign. He also sits at the top of the league's scoring chart.

Effaghe is a familiar name at SkyrScout. Scouty Pippen profiled the striker in January 2025, when he was playing for Gomel in Belarus and had recorded 17 goals and four assists in 27 league matches. The scouting assessment highlighted his acceleration, physical strength, finishing and exceptional leap, while noting that the level of competition in Belarus made the next step an important test.

His latest performance offers a particularly fitting reminder of that early assessment: the winning goal came from precisely the kind of powerful headed finish Pippen had identified as one of his standout qualities. With nine goals already this season, Effaghe continues to make his mark at a higher level.
