Sylla stands out as an explosive ball carrier with outstanding close control, flair and acceleration. The powerful 186 cm midfielder combines athleticism and technical quality with a dangerous left foot, creative passing and well-timed late runs into the penalty area.

He scored five league goals during a productive loan spell with BFC Daugavpils before earning a recall to RFS, where he has already made an impact in domestic and European competition.
