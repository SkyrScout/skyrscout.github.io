Eduards Dašķevičs has scored his **first senior international goal for Latvia**, finding the net in Monday's UEFA Nations League match away to Cyprus.

The 24-year-old started for Latvia and broke the deadlock in the 57th minute with a superb individual effort.

Vladislavs Gutkovskis found Dašķevičs on the left before the winger carried the ball across the pitch and into the penalty area, worked it through the legs of a defender and curled a left-footed finish into the far top corner.

It was Dašķevičs' first goal for the Latvia senior national team and gave his side a 1-0 lead in a competitive international.

Latvia were unable to hold on. Ioannis Kosti equalised in the 70th minute before Marcus Edwards struck in the fourth minute of stoppage time to give Cyprus a 2-1 victory.

The result was painful for Latvia, but the night still brought a personal landmark for Dašķevičs.
