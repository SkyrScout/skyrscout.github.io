Erbol Atabaev scored the winning goal as Lokomotiva Zagreb beat Slaven Belupo 2-1 in the Croatian top flight on Saturday, producing an impressive display in his new league.

The visitors took an early lead through Lovro Banovec in the third minute, but Mirko Marić equalised just six minutes later. Atabaev settled the contest in the 67th minute with a left-footed strike from outside the penalty area.

The 25-year-old Kyrgyz attacking midfielder offered more than the decisive goal. Playing in an advanced role, he created a big chance, showed his ability to take on defenders and finished with a Sofascore rating of 8.1 before being substituted in the 83rd minute.

Scout Pilgrim profiled Atabaev for SkyrScout in July 2026, when he was excelling for Muras United in Kyrgyzstan. The report highlighted his vision, technique, two-footed ability and eye for goal, while questioning whether he could produce the same quality in a stronger league. This performance in Croatia is an encouraging sign, even if the longer-term test is still ahead.
