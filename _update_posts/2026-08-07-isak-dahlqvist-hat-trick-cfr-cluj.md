Isak Dahlqvist scored a hat-trick as Tromsø produced a dominant 5–0 away victory over CFR Cluj in the first leg of the UEFA Conference League third qualifying round.

Dahlqvist, operating at right wing-back, found the net in the 27th, 61st and 73rd minutes as Tromsø took a commanding advantage back to Norway ahead of the return leg.

The Swedish wide player has experience in both attacking roles and at wing-back, and delivered one of the standout individual performances of the qualifying round with three goals on the European stage.
