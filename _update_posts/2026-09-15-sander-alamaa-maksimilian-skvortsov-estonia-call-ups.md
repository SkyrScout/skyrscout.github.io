Sander Alamaa and Maksimilian Skvortsov have both received their first call-ups to the Estonia senior national team.

The two 18-year-olds have been selected in Jürgen Henn's squad for the upcoming international window, giving Estonia two potential senior debutants from the A. Le Coq Premium Liiga.

Both players have been profiled by SkyrScout.

### Alamaa rewarded for his breakthrough season

Alamaa has established himself as one of the standout young attacking players in Estonia with Flora Tallinn.

The 2008-born winger has become a regular first-team contributor and has already made a significant impact in the Premium Liiga despite still being only 18.

SkyrScout profiled Alamaa as a high-upside wide attacker with pace, directness and the ability to create danger in 1v1 situations.

### Skvortsov continues rapid rise

Skvortsov's call-up follows an impressive season with FCI Levadia.

The 2007-born midfielder has developed into an important first-team player and has continued to contribute goals and assists while competing at the top end of the Premium Liiga.

SkyrScout profiled Skvortsov in July 2026, highlighting his ball-carrying, movement, creativity and ability to influence games from midfield.

The first senior call-up is another major step in a rapid rise for both players.
