Emrick Fotsing made a superb start to the 2026 U20 CONCACAF Championship, captaining Canada and scoring twice in a 3–0 victory over Panama in their opening Group C match in Puebla, Mexico.

The 2007-born Vancouver FC midfielder needed just nine minutes to make his mark. According to [AS USA](https://as.com/us/futbol/canada-pasa-por-encima-de-panama-en-el-premundial-sub-20-f202607-n/), Fotsing rose to head home from a corner to give Canada the lead. Liam Mackenzie converted a penalty five minutes later before Fotsing struck again in second-half stoppage time to complete both his brace and Canada's 3–0 win.

It was an impressive opening statement in an important tournament for Canada's emerging generation. [Canada Soccer](https://news.canadasoccer.com/canada-soccer-names-squad-for-2026-concacaf-u-20-championship) named Fotsing among the 21 players selected for the championship, where Canada have been drawn alongside Panama, Honduras and Jamaica. The four semifinalists will qualify for the 2027 FIFA U-20 World Cup, while the tournament also carries implications for qualification to the 2028 Olympic Games.

Fotsing's performance comes as little surprise to those who have followed his rapid development. SkyrScout took a closer look at the Vancouver FC midfielder in November 2025 and highlighted his strength, athleticism and ability to influence the game at both ends of the pitch.

Our scouting report described him as a powerful box-to-box midfielder, capable of carrying the ball forward at speed, making well-timed runs into attacking areas and providing a genuine goal threat from midfield. His physical strength, aerial presence and timing were also identified as major qualities – attributes that were on display again when he headed Canada into the lead against Panama.

His progress has since attracted wider recognition in Canada. Ahead of the 2026 Canadian Premier League season, the [Canadian Premier League](https://www.cplsoccer.com/news/eight-u-21-canadians-to-watch-during-the-2026-cpl-season) selected Fotsing among eight U21 players to watch. After his first professional season with Vancouver FC, in which he made 26 appearances across all competitions and scored four goals, the league identified him as one of the CPL's most promising young midfield talents.

Now captaining his country and opening the U20 CONCACAF Championship with two goals, Fotsing has provided another strong indication of why he caught our attention last autumn.
