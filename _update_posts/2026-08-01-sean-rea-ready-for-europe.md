Sean Rea is earning well-deserved praise for an MVP-calibre season with FC Supra du Québec.

OneSoccer highlighted the 24-year-old's performances and asked whether he could reach new heights in MLS. At SkyrScout, we have long felt that Rea is hugely underrated.

Our scouting report from autumn 2025 highlighted a technically gifted, two-footed playmaker with brilliant close control, vision and ball-carrying ability. He can unlock defences from open play, create danger from set pieces and provide the kind of individual flair that is increasingly difficult to find.

But rather than see him become stuck in the MLS system, we would love to see the artist take his game to Europe.

In the right club, under a coach who values technical creators and gives them genuine responsibility, Rea has the qualities to become much more than a useful squad player.

Is the artist ready to leave North America behind?
