The list of clubs interested in Kazakhstan international Zhasulan Amir continues to grow, with interest now also being reported from MLS and New York Red Bulls.

Scout in Eurasia reports that Ordabasy have received offers from Baltika Kaliningrad and İstanbul Başakşehir for the 2006-born forward. Club president Darkhan Kyzaibayev has also indicated that Amir remains focused on helping Ordabasy in their fight for the league title before deciding his future.

The latest developments follow previous reports of interest from Zulte Waregem, whose €500,000 proposal was reportedly rejected.
