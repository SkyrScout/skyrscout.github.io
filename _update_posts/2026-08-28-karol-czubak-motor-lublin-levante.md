Motor Lublin have signalled that they have no intention of entertaining offers for Karol Czubak as speculation continues over interest from Levante.

Czubak was linked with the La Liga club earlier this month, with Spanish journalist Eric Martín Gasulla reporting that the Polish striker is one of several forwards being monitored by Levante.

There has still been no report of a formal bid or negotiations between the clubs, but Motor have now publicly addressed the possibility of losing their leading striker before the transfer window closes.

Speaking ahead of Motor's match against Piast Gliwice, head coach Mariusz Misiura said the club's owner has made his position clear.

According to Misiura, the owner has stated that Motor will not consider offers for the club's leading players, leaving the coach confident that Czubak will remain in Lublin.

The 26-year-old scored 18 league goals during the 2025/26 season and has continued his form into the new campaign, scoring three times in his opening five Ekstraklasa appearances. He also made his senior Poland debut in June.

Czubak's rise will be familiar to long-time SkyrScout followers. We first profiled the striker in December 2024 during his time with Arka Gdynia, having followed him before the launch of the channel.

For now, Levante's reported interest remains just that, while Motor's public stance suggests it would take a significant development for Czubak to leave before the window closes.
