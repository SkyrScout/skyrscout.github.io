Emrick Fotsing registered an assist as Canada drew 1–1 with Honduras in their second match of the Concacaf U-20 Championship.

The 2007-born midfielder set up Lucas Lima's 57th-minute opener, taking his tournament tally to two goals and one assist after two matches.
