Layan Loor has received a notable piece of external recognition, with the CIES Football Observatory ranking the Independiente del Valle left-back as the **best-performing wide back in South America**.

Loor tops the CIES list with a performance index of **78.9**, ahead of River Plate's Marcos Acuña on 78.4. The ranking covers wide defenders across the continent rather than separating left- and right-backs.

The CIES performance index is data-driven and uses information from Impect, evaluating several areas of a player's contribution rather than relying on traditional journalist ratings. The model takes into account actions including chance creation, progression with the ball, build-up play, defensive work and other aspects of performance.

The timing is interesting for another reason.

Bolavip reports, citing information from Nicolás Ayala with FedePostera, that new Ecuador head coach **Marcelo Gallardo is considering Loor as an option for the national team**. No call-up has been announced, so this remains a reported possibility rather than a confirmed selection.

Gallardo already knows the player from direct opposition. Loor started for Independiente del Valle when they faced Gallardo's River Plate in the 2025 Copa Libertadores, a match that finished 2-2 in Quito.

Loor has previously been involved with Ecuador at senior level, including a call-up in 2024, meaning a place in Gallardo's first cycle would be a recall rather than a first introduction to La Tri.

For a player already on the SkyrScout radar, the two developments make for an interesting combination: a continent-wide performance model currently places him at the top of his positional group, while his name is also being discussed around Ecuador's new national-team setup.

No call-up yet. But being ranked first among South America's wide backs is not the worst way to get a new national coach's attention.
