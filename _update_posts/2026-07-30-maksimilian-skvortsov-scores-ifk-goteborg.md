Maksimilian Skvortsov scored his first European goal as FCI Levadia were eliminated by IFK Göteborg in the UEFA Conference League second qualifying round.

Levadia entered the home leg with a 2–1 advantage, but trailed 1–0 after 90 minutes before eventually losing 3–1 after extra time and 4–3 on aggregate.

The 2007-born midfielder came off the bench and scored in the final moments of extra time, providing a small consolation for Levadia at the end of their European campaign.
