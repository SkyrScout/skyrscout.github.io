Fran Topić delivered a timely reminder of his quality with two second-half goals as Croatia U21 came from behind to beat Germany U21 3-2 in Rijeka.

Croatia led through Adrian Segečić before Germany turned the match around after the break, with Max Moerstedt and Dženan Pejčinović making it 2-1.

Topić then took over.

The 22-year-old made it 2-2 in the 67th minute before scoring again in the 84th to complete the comeback and secure an impressive victory against one of Europe's strongest U21 sides.

The performance comes at an interesting point in Topić's season. Since returning to Dinamo Zagreb, regular club minutes have been difficult to come by. He has started only a small number of league matches this season, and in September Croatian media reported that Dinamo were considering a loan after leaving him out of their Europa League squad.

Against Germany, however, Topić was decisive when Croatia needed him most.

SkyrScout profiled Topić during his loan spell at Zrinjski Mostar in January 2025, highlighting his two-footed finishing, efficient dribbling, physical presence and ability to operate as an inverted winger or wide forward.

Two goals and the match-winner against Germany U21 is a significant response during a challenging period at club level.
