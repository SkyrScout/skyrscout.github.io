Thierry Gale has finished a remarkable international run with **six goals and two assists in four matches for Barbados** across the September-October Concacaf Nations League window.

The 24-year-old was directly involved in eight goals as Barbados collected three wins and a draw from four League B matches.

Gale opened the run against Saint Lucia on 25 September, scoring from the penalty spot before setting up Niall Reid-Stephen for the second goal in a 2-0 victory.

Three days later, he scored again as Barbados drew 2-2 with Guadeloupe, curling home the equaliser in the second half.

The numbers then accelerated against Bermuda.

Gale scored twice and assisted Reid-Stephen in a 4-1 win on 2 October, before producing another brace in the return fixture three days later. Barbados trailed 1-0 late in that match, but Gale scored twice in quick succession to turn the game around and secure a 2-1 victory.

That leaves his four-match return at:

**4 appearances  
6 goals  
2 assists  
8 goal contributions**

SkyrScout first featured Gale as part of the *Scout America* series, highlighting his pace, finishing ability, shooting and threat from set pieces.

Four international matches later, the Barbados winger has provided a fairly emphatic reminder of that attacking output.
