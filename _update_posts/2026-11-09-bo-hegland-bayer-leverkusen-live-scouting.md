Bayer Leverkusen have reportedly watched Bo Hegland live as the list of major European clubs monitoring the Djurgården midfielder continues to grow.

According to Expressen, via Fotbolltransfers and Fotbollskanalen, representatives from the Bundesliga club were in attendance when Djurgården beat Degerfors 2-1. Leverkusen were reportedly scouting both Hegland and Norwegian striker Kristian Lien.

There is currently no bid for either player, but the report adds another significant name to the list of clubs linked with Hegland.

The 22-year-old has enjoyed a remarkable 2026 season, with 11 goals and 17 assists for Djurgården. Arsenal, Barcelona, Newcastle and Anderlecht have all previously been linked with the Norwegian midfielder.

For now, the Leverkusen development belongs firmly in the **live scouting** category rather than concrete transfer negotiations. But with several major European clubs now reported to have watched or followed Hegland, interest ahead of the winter window appears to be building.

SkyrScout first profiled Hegland while he was at Moss in June 2025, highlighting his technical quality, vision, finishing and set-piece ability.
