Maksimilian Skvortsov has been named in the **Estonia senior squad again** for the final two matches of the UEFA Nations League group stage.

The 18-year-old FCI Levadia midfielder is part of Jürgen Henn's 25-man squad for the away match against Luxembourg on 13 November and the home fixture against Bulgaria three days later.

Skvortsov received his first senior call-up in September and went on to make his international debut against Iceland on 6 October, coming off the bench in a 0-0 Nations League draw.

The Estonian FA now lists him with **one senior cap**, and his inclusion for the November window gives him another opportunity to add to that total.

SkyrScout profiled Skvortsov earlier this year after his breakthrough with Levadia, highlighting his technical quality, progressive carrying and ability to influence games from midfield.

A first call-up became a first cap.

Now he is back for more.
