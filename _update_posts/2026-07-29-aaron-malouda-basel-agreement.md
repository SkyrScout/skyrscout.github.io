FC Basel and Sabah FK have reached an agreement for the transfer of Aaron Malouda.

According to L'Équipe, the two clubs have finalised a deal estimated at €3 million for the 20-year-old French winger, who now looks set to continue his career in Switzerland.

The agreement follows Basel's final offer earlier this month and brings Malouda closer to a return to Western European football after his successful spell with Sabah FK in Azerbaijan.

Malouda has enjoyed a breakthrough season, recording 9 goals and 4 assists in 29 matches.
