Experimental prototype

SkyrScout Card Game Beta

Player cards are generated from SkyrScout profiles. The experimental game engine combines structured scouting facts, supported profile traits and SkyrScout video performance — with missing data always giving no effect.

Choose your Scout

Your Scout will affect the team later. In this Beta, the choice is saved for the draft but has no gameplay bonus yet.

What do I do? Choose one Scout. After that, the game will draw three player cards at a time.

Step 2 of 2 · Draft

Choose 1 of 3

Pick one player to add to your team.

How the draft works Your five slots are Defender → Midfielder → Attacker → Wildcard → Wildcard. For each slot, three eligible players are drawn. Choose one; the other two are discarded.
