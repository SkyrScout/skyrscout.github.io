  The main concern is not his basic skill set but whether his body can withstand the demands of regular senior football over time. Jah had returned from a serious knee injury and was still rebuilding rhythm and consistency. He was also not yet an established starter at Floriana despite his strong goalscoring output.

  Career trajectory and current form

  Nicknamed "Zlatan" in Gambia, Jah moved to CFR Cluj in Romania in the summer of 2022 and looked ready to take a significant step forward before suffering an ACL injury late in 2023. He returned to training around a year later, but left Cluj in the summer of 2025 and signed a one-year contract with Floriana FC in Malta.

  His early performances in Malta showed why he remains an interesting striker. The combination of size, pace, aerial ability, movement and finishing gives him a profile that is difficult to find, while his 6 goals in 10 matches demonstrated that he could still produce efficiently after the long injury layoff.
