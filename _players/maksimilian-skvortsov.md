  Maksimilian Skvortsov | 18-year-old midfielder | Estonia

  Maksimilian Skvortsov is an 18-year-old central midfielder playing for FCI Levadia in the Estonian Premium Liiga. Standing 178 cm tall, he primarily operates as a number eight, usually in the right half-space but equally comfortable on the left.

  Despite not being physically imposing, he is strong through the hips and has a low centre of gravity and excellent balance. This allows him to shield the ball well and compete aggressively in midfield.

  One of the first things that stands out is his intensity. Skvortsov combines quick feet with an excellent work rate and covers large areas of the pitch. He is particularly impressive in ground duels, winning 6.6 per 90 minutes with a 51% success rate according to SofaScore.

  Although not a flashy dribbler, he has clean close control, escapes pressure well and carries the ball confidently through midfield.

  His football intelligence is exceptional for his age. Constantly scanning before receiving possession, he plays with a composure that makes him look far more experienced than 18.

  Predominantly right-footed, Skvortsov regularly breaks defensive lines with perfectly weighted through balls, averaging an outstanding 1.9 key passes per game. He also delivers dangerous crosses from open play and set pieces.

  The young Estonian provides genuine attacking output. He contributes with both goals and assists, often arriving late into the penalty area with excellent timing. His finishing with his stronger right foot is composed, while he has also shown that he can attack crosses effectively, highlighted by an outstanding headed goal against Nõmme United.

  His right foot is a major weapon. Whether shooting from distance, delivering corners and free-kicks or taking penalties, he consistently produces quality.

  Many young midfielders stand out because of one outstanding attribute. Skvortsov's greatest strength may be his consistency. Whether defending, progressing play, creating chances or arriving in the box, he performs every aspect of the game to a remarkably high level for his age.

  Areas for improvement

  Like any young midfielder, Skvortsov still has areas to refine.

  Confident in his shooting ability, he occasionally pulls the trigger too early instead of recognising a better passing option. Averaging 2.5 shots per game while only 0.6 hit the target suggests there is room for better decision-making.

  His aerial game could also improve. Although his height naturally limits him somewhat, better timing and aggression would make him an even more complete midfielder, particularly in more physical leagues.

  Adding more penetrative runs beyond the defensive line would also diversify his game. Much of his attacking output currently comes from intelligent late runs into the box.
