  <h3>League format</h3>

  The league consists of 16 teams and is played across two tournaments (Apertura and Clausura). Both Apertura finalists earn international berths: the winners qualify directly for the Copa Libertadores group stage, while the runners-up secure a Copa Sudamericana spot.

  The Torneo Clausura is a double round-robin, where the 16 teams play each other twice for a total of 30 matches. The team at the top of the table after 30 rounds is crowned champion and qualifies for the Copa Libertadores group stage. (Source: Wikipedia)
