  Andrews Appiah | 18-year-old Attacker | Ghana

  Andrews Appiah is an 18-year-old attacker currently playing for FK Auda in the Latvian Virsliga. Standing at just 170 cm, he may initially look small and unassuming, but he is surprisingly strong on his feet, with a low centre of gravity and excellent balance.

  What immediately catches the eye is his exceptional acceleration and pace over distance, combined with excellent close control and dribbling ability. Appiah is also a natural goalscorer, with intelligent movement inside the penalty area and very good finishing ability. He strikes the ball cleanly and is comfortable finishing with either foot. Despite his height, he has even found the net with a header this season, although aerial play is naturally not his strongest attribute.

  Appiah can operate on either flank as well as through the middle, although his role as a centre-forward is perhaps better described as that of a false nine. When deployed up front, he tends to drop deeper and operate primarily in the left half-space. He is constantly on the move, finding pockets of space and combining effectively with his teammates in the final third. His passing is very good, and he shows impressive anticipation and vision when linking up with others.

  When his teammates are unable to find him between the lines, he is equally willing to make well-timed runs beyond the defensive line. With his lightning speed, he is extremely difficult for defenders to keep up with once he gets going.

  He is also capable of producing powerful strikes from distance. Predominantly right-footed, his left foot is no less impressive when it comes to generating power. Appiah plays with considerable self-confidence and is never afraid to take on his opponent or try again after a failed attempt. That willingness to keep taking responsibility is an appealing part of his game.

  Areas for improvement

  Despite his obvious talent, Appiah is still very young and has several areas in which he can improve. One of the most important is his decision-making, particularly when choosing between shooting and passing. Given his shooting ability with both feet, it is understandable that he is tempted to pull the trigger whenever he gets within range of goal. However, there are situations where he would be better served by taking an extra touch, looking for a teammate or choosing a different option.

  A player with his physical and technical attributes should also be capable of contributing more assists. That said, he is still getting to know his teammates, and better understanding of their movements and preferences should help him become more productive in this department. It is also worth noting that some of the chances he has created could arguably have resulted in assists with better finishing from his teammates.

  Finally, while his high pressing is aggressive and his work rate is good, there is room to improve the execution. With some refinement in his pressing angles, timing and coordination with teammates, he could become considerably more effective without necessarily having to work harder.

  Career trajectory and current form

  Andrews Appiah's career is not the easiest to follow, but according to Transfermarkt, he came through the ranks at Ghana Division One League club PAC Academy FC before joining the academy of Ghana Premier League side Bechem United in August 2022. He is subsequently credited with making his senior debut for Bechem in the Ghana Premier League in September 2022, at just 14 years old.

  In January 2024, he returned to PAC Academy FC, where he presumably gained regular first-team experience. Unfortunately, there are no reliable official statistics available from the Ghanaian second tier. Regarded as one of Ghana's bright young talents and a member of the Ghana U17 side, the Black Starlets, Appiah eventually caught the attention of FK Auda. The Latvian club's excellent scouting department secured his signature on a free transfer at the end of June 2026.

  It did not take long for Appiah to settle in Latvia. He initially featured on the left wing, but when FK Auda sold their young, 196 cm centre-forward to KAA Gent for €3 million, Appiah was given an opportunity through the middle. The two players could hardly be more different. Where Vergara was a physically imposing target man, Appiah offers a much more mobile, technically oriented false-nine profile.

  Despite having to adapt to a different role, Appiah made a significant impact. FK Auda progressed to the third qualifying round of the UEFA Conference League and finished third in the domestic league. The young Ghanaian ended the campaign with seven goals and one assist in 17 appearances, impressing Latvian supporters, local observers and, undoubtedly, a number of scouts from higher-ranked European leagues.

  Transfer outlook

From that position, he could make full use of his exceptional acceleration and pace, combined with his close control and dribbling ability. Cutting inside onto his stronger right foot would allow him to exploit his excellent shooting ability, while also creating opportunities for teammates.

  From a development perspective, another season in the Latvian Virsliga would probably be beneficial. However, given his talent and the impression he has already made, I would be surprised if he were not playing in a significantly higher-ranked league by the summer of 2027. There are several leagues in which his profile could be an excellent fit.
