  Edgar Piloyan | 21-year-old Defensive Midfielder | Armenia

  Edgar Piloyan is a 21-year-old defensive midfielder standing 179 cm tall. He may not be the tallest, but he is strong on his feet, tough in shoulder-to-shoulder duels and possesses an excellent work rate, with the stamina to sustain an aggressive pressing game for the full 90 minutes. He can operate as either a No. 6 or No. 8, but his natural role is as a holding midfielder. He can, however, step into a more attacking box-to-box role when required.

  Predominantly right-footed, Piloyan can strike the ball with serious power from distance, while also being very capable with his left. His two-footedness is particularly valuable in possession. He is comfortable playing shorter build-up passes from deep and helping set the tempo, but can also switch play with longer passes, deliver crosses and combine effectively in the final third. His passing accuracy is consistently high, and he has the technical ability to find teammates with key passes from a variety of positions.

  Though a clear defensive midfielder, he's surprisingly creative for his role. His close control and dribbling allow him to escape pressure, while he is also a very capable ball carrier at speed. This gives him the ability to turn defensive recoveries into attacking transitions, making his attacking contribution significant.

  The first thing that really stands out, however, is his ability to read the game. Piloyan anticipates passes extremely well and has a knack for stepping in at exactly the right moment to intercept or recover possession. His aggression and anticipation allow him to win well over 50% of his ground duels, yet he often makes these recoveries look remarkably simple, almost as if he can simply run up to an opponent and take the ball away from them.

  Another interesting aspect of his game is his movement into the box. Piloyan makes well-timed late runs, either creating space for attacking teammates or arriving in positions where he can contribute himself. His stamina and mentality become particularly valuable in close-fought matches, where he can still produce a powerful run from midfield late in the game. On several occasions, this can even see him overlap an attacker on the wing and deliver a dangerous cross in the dying minutes.

  One final quality worth highlighting is his consistency. His floor is high, and even when he is not at his best, he never disappears from games or produces a genuinely poor performance. That reliability is an important part of what makes him an interesting prospect for the next level.
