  Gabriel Antinoro | 21-year-old Canadian-Brazilian wide player

  A dynamic and versatile player capable of operating on both flanks as a winger or advanced wingback. The wingback role appears best suited to his long-term development, particularly on the left side.

  There are shades of former Rosenborg and Liverpool fullback Vegard Heggem in his style: direct, athletic and attack-minded.

  Antinoro combines agility, pace and intelligent movement with a strong technical base. Comfortable in tight spaces, he excels at quick one-touch passing and linking play through midfield.

  His right foot delivers quality crosses and powerful strikes, while his left remains a reliable secondary option. He is effective when carrying the ball forward at speed and contributes both goals and assists through his proactive play.

  Defensively, Antinoro shows commitment and good timing in ground duels. He reads the game well and is disciplined enough to handle the deeper responsibilities required of a wingback.

  At 173 cm, he is not dominant in the air but competes bravely when required.

  Areas for improvement

  There is relatively little to criticise at this level. The next challenge is to demonstrate that his tempo, composure and technical quality can translate successfully to a stronger league.

  Transfer outlook

  Antinoro's versatility, technical quality and suitability for an advanced wingback role make him ready for a bigger stage.
