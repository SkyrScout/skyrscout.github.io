  Jaime Peralta | 21-year-old Centre-Forward | Colombia

  Jaime Peralta is a 185 cm tall, big, strong and athletic centre-forward with good acceleration and very good pace. His best position is as a typical number 9, where he can make full use of his physical qualities and goal-scoring ability.

  His hold-up play is very good and he thrives as the focal point of the attack, receiving long passes and protecting the ball while his teammates move higher up the pitch. He distributes the ball reliably with accurate passes and is difficult to dispossess when defenders are tight to him. While young centre-forwards often struggle to win aerial duels when contesting long balls from deeper areas, Peralta is already excellent in this department.

  He is also a constant problem for defenders with his well-timed runs in behind. Using his size, strength and pace, he is a handful when charging forward and chasing balls into space. His intensity in the high press is another positive, and he competes aggressively in ground duels as well.

  What stands out most, however, is his finishing. Agile despite his size, Peralta strikes the ball cleanly and with immense power, including from awkward positions, and is capable of finishing equally well with either foot. His aerial ability becomes another major weapon inside the box, where he is a constant threat when attacking crosses and set pieces.

  His technical level is more inconsistent. His first touch can range from outstanding to frustratingly sloppy, and he is not a natural dribbler or progressive ball carrier. He can hold his own when isolated, but this is not where his game is built around.

  Areas for improvement

  There are several smaller details Peralta should continue to work on. As mentioned, his first touch can be sloppy at times, but this appears less about basic technique and more about concentration and focus in the moment.

  His combination and link-up play can still improve in the final third, as can his movement inside the penalty area. He is capable of scoring spectacular goals, but becoming better at finding the simple tap-in and attacking the right spaces consistently could make his numbers even better.

  Composure under pressure is another area for development. Peralta once sent a penalty miles over the goal and almost out of the stadium. To be fair, he had won the penalty himself, and perhaps should not have been the one taking it in the first place.

  Speaking of composure, however, his biggest concern is his control of his temper and emotions. He must learn to stop arguing with referees, even when his fury may be justified. He has already been sent off twice in 18 appearances this season after receiving two yellow cards, both following confrontations with the referee. Three of his five yellow cards this season have come for dissent.

  Looking further back, Peralta has been collecting yellow cards like they were Pokémon cards. This is something he has to get under control if he wants to build a career outside South America. The physicality and aggression are valuable parts of his game; losing control and making himself unavailable is not.

  Career trajectory and current form

  Peralta developed through the academy of his hometown club Cúcuta Deportivo and made his first-team debut in July 2023, when the club was playing in Colombia's second tier.

  Recognised as a major talent, he was loaned to Independiente Medellín in Categoría Primera A in January 2024. He scored 3 goals in 10 appearances and also featured in three Copa Sudamericana matches. Despite the relative success, the loan was cut short and he returned to Cúcuta in April, where he immediately made an impact with 5 goals in 8 matches.

  His performances attracted the attention of Argentine giants Club Atlético San Lorenzo de Almagro, who brought him in on loan in January 2025. The move was less successful from a playing-time perspective, with Peralta recording just 1 goal in 96 minutes across five appearances. He returned to Cúcuta in June and helped the club secure promotion back to Colombia's top flight.

  The 2026 season has been his real breakthrough at senior level. Peralta has impressed in the Colombian first tier, scoring 8 goals in 18 matches for Cúcuta, who are understandably struggling near the bottom of the table after promotion.

  Transfer outlook

  Colombia currently sits third in CONMEBOL's league ranking, meaning Peralta is already competing at a respectable level and is under no immediate pressure to leave.

  Russian side Rubin Kazan has reportedly submitted an offer for the striker, but Cúcuta are reluctant to sell without having a replacement ready. Much will therefore depend on the size of the offer and whether Peralta considers Russia the right next step in his career.

  There is no official information available regarding his contract length, although it is reportedly running until 31 December 2027. Without knowing the exact contractual situation, it is difficult to estimate his value, but a fee in the region of €1–2 million would not be surprising when compared with similar transfers from Colombian clubs.

  From a profile perspective, Peralta looks particularly well suited to the Norwegian Eliteserien, especially for teams using an aggressive pressing approach such as Rosenborg or Brann. His physicality, athleticism, high pressing, aerial ability and goal-scoring instinct are a combination that should translate well to Scandinavian football.

  At the same time, these qualities are highly sought after across Europe. Brazilian and Argentine clubs in particular are known for vacuuming up promising South American forwards before they become too expensive, so clubs interested in Peralta should not wait too long.

  The temperament issue is a genuine concern, but it is also one of the areas where there may be the biggest room for improvement. If he can mature without losing the aggression and competitive edge that make him such a handful, Peralta has the physical and goal-scoring profile to take a significant step up from Colombia.
