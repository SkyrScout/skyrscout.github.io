  Moussa Sambe | 20-year-old Centre-Forward | Senegal

  Moussa Sambe is a 20-year-old centre-forward currently playing for Tartu JK Tammeka in the Estonian Premium Liiga, on loan from Estonian champions FCI Levadia. Standing at 187 cm, he has an extremely athletic build. What immediately catches the eye is his combination of strength, exceptional acceleration and lightning speed over distance, complemented by close control and very good dribbling ability.

  Operating as the highest player in the team, Sambe frequently drops deeper to receive the ball, where his hold-up play is another notable strength. When the midfielders are unable to find him through the middle, he often drifts towards the flanks, either to receive the ball in a one-on-one situation against the full-back or to combine quickly with his teammates. When isolated against a defender out wide, he uses his dribbling ability and explosive acceleration to get past his opponent, before either driving towards goal or reaching the byline to deliver a cross.

  He is also comfortable dropping deep to collect the ball himself when the situation calls for it. His excellent ball-carrying ability allows him to turn and drive forward, progressing the play and carrying the attack into more advanced areas.

  His passing game is very solid, with good vision and anticipation. Although predominantly right-footed, he is also very comfortable using his left, and can generate powerful shots from distance with either foot. Sambe is willing to work hard for the team, contributing effectively to the high press and showing a good instinct for intercepting passes and quickly turning defensive actions into attacking opportunities.

  Another important dimension to his game is his movement in behind the opposition defence. His runs are well timed, and combined with his lightning pace, they make him an extremely difficult player to contain over longer distances. His movement inside the penalty area is already very well developed for his age. He is particularly alert to rebounds, using his outstanding acceleration to react quickly and attack loose balls before defenders can recover. On top of that, he possesses very good finishing ability with both feet, making him a constant goal threat.

  Areas for improvement

  His biggest area for improvement is undoubtedly his aerial ability, which is currently rather poor. Given his height and athleticism, you would expect him to be much more influential in the air. At present, his contribution in aerial duels is almost non-existent, and this is an area that needs considerable work if he is to establish himself as a centre-forward at a higher level.

  Another concern is his tendency to get involved in unnecessary arguments with referees and opposition players. His complaints and confrontations have already resulted in yellow cards, including a second yellow that led to a sending-off this season. Learning to control his emotions and maintain his composure would be an important step in his development.

  Career trajectory and current form

  Moussa Felix Sambe began his professional career with ASC Niarry Tally in Dakar. In August 2025, he was signed by Estonian second-tier club Viimsi JK for an undisclosed fee, but never made an appearance for the club. Instead, he was immediately sent out on loan to then-Premium Liiga side JK Tallinna Kalev, where he made 13 appearances and scored twice.

  His athleticism and technical ability soon caught the attention of Estonian giants FCI Levadia, who signed him in January 2026, again for an undisclosed fee. Sambe initially received regular first-team opportunities, but his playing time gradually decreased. After making eight appearances without scoring, accumulating just 205 minutes, Levadia decided that a loan move would be beneficial for his development. He joined Tartu JK Tammeka in August for the remainder of the 2026 season.

  The move has proved to be an excellent decision. Sambe has exploded onto the scene at Tammeka, registering seven goals and four assists in just 13 appearances. He has also won two penalties for the club, further underlining the impact he has made since arriving.

  Transfer outlook

  Estonia currently sits 49th in the UEFA league ranking, but regular first-team football in the Premium Liiga has provided Sambe with a valuable platform to continue his development.

  Despite seeing enormous potential in him, we believe another season in Estonia could be the best next step. FCI Levadia's current top scorer, Wendell Gabriel, is likely to attract interest and could leave either in January or during the summer transfer window. If that happens, we expect Levadia to give Sambe a genuine opportunity to fill his shoes and establish himself as their first-choice centre-forward.

  According to Transfermarkt, Sambe's contract with FCI Levadia runs until 31 December 2027. However, the club announced on its own website that he signed a contract in January 2026, running until 31 December 2030. This puts Levadia in a very strong negotiating position should other clubs come calling.

  We would currently estimate his transfer value at around €400,000–500,000. If he continues his development and delivers another strong season, that figure could easily double by next summer. Even then, he could represent an excellent investment.

  In the long term, I believe Sambe could be a very interesting fit for the Norwegian Eliteserien. However, he will need to make significant improvements in the air if he is to succeed as a centre-forward in such a physically demanding league. His pace, strength and ability to carry the ball could also make him an intriguing option out wide, where his current aerial limitations would be less of a concern.

  Belgian clubs and scouts are also known to monitor the Baltic leagues closely, and I would not be surprised if Sambe's name has already found its way onto several scouting shortlists in Belgium. With his combination of physical attributes, technical ability and current goal output, he is certainly a player worth keeping a close eye on.
