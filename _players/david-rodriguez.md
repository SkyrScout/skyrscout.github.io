  David Rodríguez | 23-year-old American-Mexican midfielder

  Currently on loan at Atlético Ottawa from Atlético de San Luis, Rodríguez is a technically gifted and versatile player capable of operating both as an attacking midfielder and as a wide playmaker from the right. He is arguably the most complete creative midfielder in the Canadian Premier League.

  Rodríguez combines flair with efficiency. An excellent dribbler with close control and strong ball-carrying ability at pace, his agility makes him elusive in one-on-one situations.

  His dominant left foot consistently produces quality deliveries and poses a threat from set pieces. Although clearly left-footed, he also has a reliable right foot and has demonstrated that he can finish effectively with it.

  His vision and passing range are top-class by Canadian Premier League standards. Rodríguez consistently identifies pockets of space and plays incisive passes through compact defensive shapes.

  His off-the-ball intelligence is another major strength. He makes well-timed runs behind the defence and regularly receives the ball in dangerous positions from which he can score or create chances.

  Defensively, Rodríguez contributes more than most creative midfielders. He anticipates play well, competes strongly in ground duels and reacts quickly after winning possession, allowing him to launch counter-attacks immediately.

  Transfer outlook

  Rodríguez is currently on loan from Atlético de San Luis, and the relationship between the clubs may complicate a permanent transfer.

  Nevertheless, his technical profile, football intelligence and end product suggest that he is ready for a significant step up. A move to a top-12 European league appears realistic, although he does not yet look ready for one of Europe's top five leagues.
