  Juan Palacios | 20-year-old Left Winger | Colombia

  Juan Palacios is a 20-year-old left winger currently playing for Paide Linnameeskond in Estonia's Premium Liiga. Standing at just 169 cm, he is small in stature but somehow manages to be the first player you notice on the pitch. Born in the Colombian city of Turbo, few birthplaces could be more fitting for a player with his explosive acceleration and lightning speed over distance. Despite his size and build, he is also surprisingly strong and difficult to push off the ball.

  Many young players with this kind of pace rely heavily on their physical advantage throughout their youth careers without developing equally impressive close control and dribbling skills. That is certainly not the case with Palacios, who possesses both in abundance.

  He thrives as a traditional wide winger and is comfortable on either flank, with his genuine two-footedness allowing him to offer a threat from both sides. He puts this ability to good use in his passing game, where he also demonstrates good anticipation and vision.

  Palacios likes to drop deeper to collect the ball before driving forward with progressive carries. However, he is equally comfortable combining with midfielders to create one-on-one situations against opposing full-backs. Once isolated against a defender, he has the acceleration, close control and dribbling ability to beat his opponent before cutting inside or driving towards the byline to deliver a cross, pick out a cutback or go for goal himself.

  With his flair and technical ability, it would be tempting to involve him in every attacking move through his dribbling. What makes him more difficult to defend against, however, is his ability to change things up with well-timed runs in behind the defensive line. He does not need the ball at his feet to be dangerous, and his movement makes him considerably less predictable.

  You would not necessarily expect a winger with his profile to contribute much defensively, but Palacios is capable of initiating the high press against opposing goalkeepers and centre-backs when required. He is also surprisingly tough in ground duels, showing that there is more to his game than pace and attacking flair.

  As mentioned, Palacios is genuinely two-footed, although he favours his left when shooting from distance. Combine his speed, acceleration, dribbling and ball-carrying ability with his excellent finishing, and you have a winger who poses a constant goal threat. With six goals in his first nine matches for Paide, he has already demonstrated that he can turn his qualities into end product.

  Palacios is one of the most exciting young players in the Estonian top flight right now. One to watch!

  Areas for improvement

  There is still room to improve the accuracy and consistency of his crossing. Given how often he gets into promising positions out wide, making better use of these situations could increase his creative output.

  At 20, he also has time to refine his tactical understanding and decision-making in the final third. Learning when to take on his opponent, when to combine with teammates and when to release the ball will be important as he moves to a higher level, where the time and space available to him will inevitably decrease.

  Career trajectory and current form

  Juan Sebastián Palacios Murillo began his youth career at Estrellas 2000 in Turbo, Antioquia before moving to Rojo FC in Medellín. The latter club combines football development with social work, helping young talents from less privileged backgrounds access opportunities in the game.

  His performances at Rojo FC attracted the attention of Brazilian giants Fluminense, who signed him in January 2024. Palacios spent the following two and a half years in the club's academy and youth setup, gaining valuable experience in one of South America's most respected development environments.

  One of the highlights of his time at Fluminense came in Chile, where he scored the winning goal in the final of the Leonel Sánchez Lineros tournament as Fluminense U18 defeated Chile U18 1–0 in October 2024.

  In August 2026, Palacios joined Estonian title contenders Paide Linnameeskond on a free transfer. The move has already proven to be an excellent opportunity for him to make the transition into senior European football. He has made an immediate impact, scoring six goals in his first nine appearances and impressing with his individual qualities in the Estonian top flight.

  Transfer outlook

  Estonia currently sits 49th in the UEFA league ranking, but the move to Paide has provided Palacios with exactly what he needed at this stage: regular first-team football in Europe and the opportunity to demonstrate his abilities against senior opposition.

  Although he has played only a handful of matches, his performances are the kind that attract attention from scouts across Europe. Ideally, I would like to see him remain at Paide until at least the summer transfer window, allowing him to build on his promising start and demonstrate that he can sustain this level. However, given the scarcity of players with his profile, some clubs may decide to move as early as January to get ahead of the competition.

  These types of players are few and far between. The combination of lightning speed, acceleration, dribbling, progressive ball carrying, genuine two-footedness, goal-scoring ability and a willingness to contribute defensively makes Palacios a particularly interesting prospect.

  We believe he would be an excellent fit for a 4-3-3 system and could suit the Norwegian Eliteserien. The jump in level would be significant, however, and a move to an Eliteserien club with the option of a loan to the OBOS-ligaen could be a sensible route. Equally, a club in the Norwegian second tier might see the opportunity to sign him directly, give him a prominent role and potentially benefit from his development before selling him on.

  It is difficult to estimate what Paide would demand to let him leave, but we believe a transfer fee in the region of €500,000–600,000 could be plausible.

  That could represent very good value for money. Palacios is still at an early stage of his senior career, but his combination of athleticism, technique and end product gives him a very high ceiling. If his development continues at the current rate, I would not rule out the possibility of him eventually reaching a Top 5 league.
