  Mirza Mulac | 21-year-old Centre-Back | Norway

  Mirza Mulac is a 21-year-old centre-back currently playing for IL Hødd in the Norwegian second tier, OBOS-ligaen. Standing at an imposing 195 cm, he combines a formidable physical presence with considerable strength. Operating as the right-sided centre-back in a back four, one of the first things that stands out is his dominance in aerial duels. He averages 3.4 aerial duels won per game, with a success rate of 65%. Add 5.3 ground duels won per game at 60%, and you have a young centre-back with some very intriguing defensive qualities.

  His pace and acceleration are decent, although they are not among his greatest strengths. His positioning, on the other hand, is very good, allowing him to compensate for his lack of exceptional speed by reading situations early and being in the right place at the right time.

  Another major strength is his passing game. Mulac has a very good passing range and, although predominantly right-footed, is perfectly capable of playing accurate short and medium-range passes with both feet. He is comfortable in possession, shows good close control and rarely looks uncomfortable when receiving the ball under pressure. He also reads the game well defensively, frequently stepping in to intercept passes and prevent potentially dangerous situations from developing.

  His attitude and willingness to put his body on the line for the team deserve special mention. On three separate occasions this season, he has thrown himself into last-ditch blocks to clear the ball virtually on the goal line. Unfortunately, two of those situations still ended in goals, but certainly not for a lack of effort from Mulac. This says a lot about his mentality and commitment to the team.

  Finally, he is a constant threat at attacking set pieces throughout the season. His height, strength and ability to attack the ball make him a dangerous presence in the opposition penalty area, and he finishes the campaign with three goals from set pieces.

  Areas for improvement

  There are still several aspects of his game that Mulac needs to work on. At times, he can be caught ball-watching when reacting to rebounds, or find himself in the right position without making the necessary physical contact with an opponent, allowing attackers to get a relatively free finish.

  He also makes a few too many errors that lead to shots on goal. It should be noted that some of these situations are a reflection of the team's overall defensive performance rather than individual mistakes. Nevertheless, there are occasions when he could do better, particularly when clearing the ball. Getting it further away from danger, or directing it towards the wider areas rather than back into central positions, would help reduce unnecessary pressure.

  Given his technical ability and comfort in possession, I would also like to see him become more ambitious with his ball carrying. He has the control and physical presence to step forward and break opposition lines more frequently, rather than always opting for the safer pass.

  Most of these are areas that should be possible to improve with the right coaching and a higher standard of daily training. At just 21 years old, Mulac is still relatively young for a centre-back and has plenty of time to develop.

  The most obvious issue, however, is his disciplinary record. He picks up far too many yellow cards for complaining to or arguing with referees. Seven yellow cards in 2025, followed by another seven yellow cards and a sending-off for two bookings in 2026, is simply not good enough. The majority of these cards could have been avoided, and he needs to learn to control his temper. I share his frustration with the standard of Norwegian refereeing at times, but losing his composure and putting himself at risk of suspension is not helping either him or his team.

  Career trajectory and current form

  Mirza Mulac was born in Norway after his parents arrived as refugees from Bosnia-Herzegovina during the war. He holds dual citizenship and is eligible to represent both countries. He began playing football at his local club, IL Hødd, as a child and worked his way through the ranks before making his first-team debut in OBOS-ligaen in April 2023, at just 18 years old.

  Hødd were relegated to the third tier after the 2023 season, but this gave the young Mulac an opportunity to establish himself as a regular starter. The club immediately bounced back, securing promotion to OBOS-ligaen with a comfortable margin. They finished the 2025 season in 10th place, with Mulac continuing to deliver promising performances.

  Ahead of the 2026 season, Hødd had ambitions of challenging for the promotion play-offs. In an attempt to become more attack-minded, they changed their formation from a back three to a back four. Mulac initially started the season at right-back but was soon moved into his more natural centre-back position on the right side of the defence. He has since put together some very impressive performances and has, at times, been among the best defensive players in the league.
