  Will Dobson | 18-year-old central midfielder | Australia

  A highly promising young midfielder capable of operating as a box-to-box No. 8 or in a more advanced central role. Despite his age, Dobson already shows maturity in possession, with a very good passing game on his right foot and an impressive ability to read the game and spot progressive options.

  Standing at 170 cm, he compensates for his size with a low centre of gravity, strong balance, and surprising physical resilience. Technically sound and comfortable under pressure, he carries the ball well through midfield and poses a real threat with late, well-timed runs into the box and in behind the defensive line. He contributes directly in the final third as both a scorer and creator, underlining his attacking instincts from midfield.

  Dobson works hard off the ball, wins his share of ground duels, and is not afraid to engage physically or step into tackles. While aerial duels are not a strength due to his stature, he competes adequately and shows good timing when required.

  Areas for improvement

  As with most young midfielders, there is room for growth across all phases of the game. Further development in high-intensity pressing and consistency in ground duels would elevate his overall impact, especially at higher levels.

  Career trajectory and current form

  A home-grown product of the Newcastle United Jets academy, Dobson made his senior debut last season and used those appearances to gain valuable experience. In the current campaign, he has taken a clear step forward, recording 3 goals and 3 assists in his first 12 matches, evidence of growing confidence and responsibility.

  Transfer outlook

  With the Australian A-League currently ranked 10th in the AFC league rankings and Dobson under contract until June 30, 2028, there is no immediate pressure to move. However, his dynamic midfield profile, technical base, and end product suggest he would adapt well to European football. Scandinavia, in particular, appears a logical and realistic next step within a top-20 UEFA league pathway.
