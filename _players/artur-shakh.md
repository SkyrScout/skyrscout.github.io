  Artur Shakh | 21-year-old Attacking Midfielder | Ukraine

  Artur Shakh is a 21-year-old attacking midfielder standing at 175 cm and currently playing for Lechia Gdańsk in the Polish second tier. Although he is not the tallest, his compact, strong build and low centre of gravity work in his favour. Combine very good pace and acceleration with excellent close control, and you get a standout progressive ball carrier who can keep driving forward throughout the full 90 minutes. In terms of player type, he reminds me of Eden Hazard.

  Shakh is often listed as a centre-forward or second striker in the starting lineup, and that is technically correct when Lechia are defending. He is frequently the first player tasked with initiating the press. In possession, however, his role looks quite different. He often drops deep to collect the ball and is excellent at finding pockets of space in the left half-space. From there, it is full speed ahead. He can carry the ball 20, 30 or even 40 metres, using his outstanding dribbling, shifts of weight, quick touches and the occasional La Croqueta to beat opponents and drive his team up the pitch.

  His anticipation and reading of the game help him find those pockets of space to receive the ball, but they also benefit his defensive work. He reads where the ball is likely to go, steps in to intercept passes and wins a fair share of his ground duels. His clever runs in behind the defensive line add another dimension to his game, making him more difficult to track and preventing opponents from simply marking him out of the match.

  Predominantly right-footed, Shakh is also capable with his left. His right foot can produce powerful, accurate strikes from distance, as shown by the three goals he has scored from outside the box in just eight appearances this season.

  Beyond his ball-carrying ability, what really stands out is Shakh's outstanding goal threat. His intelligent movement inside the box makes him difficult to pick up, and he has shown excellent finishing with either foot. He has also been highly productive creatively, recording five assists in his first eight appearances. The combination of goals, assists and the ability to create danger by carrying the ball makes him one of the most exciting breakout players in the division so far.

  Areas for improvement

  It feels strange to criticise a player who has already recorded five assists in eight matches, but I still believe there is room for improvement in his passing. He can look to increase both his volume of accurate passes and his effectiveness when combining or playing key passes in the final third. Given how often he gets into promising positions, improving these details could make him an even more complete attacking player.

  At 175 cm, aerial dominance is not something you would necessarily expect from Shakh. Nevertheless, this is an area where he can continue to improve. He deserves credit for already winning several important aerial duels that have had a direct impact on matches.

  Career trajectory and current form

  Artur Romanovich Shakh began his youth career at Polissya Zhytomyr before moving to Karpaty Lviv's youth setup, UFK-Karpaty. In the summer of 2022, he stepped up to the first team and made his debut in August against FSK Mariupol.

  During the 2023/24 season, he helped Karpaty secure promotion to the Ukrainian Premier League. However, over the following two seasons, he rarely established himself as a regular starter. Lechia Gdańsk, looking to bounce straight back to the Ekstraklasa after relegation, identified Shakh as a player who could help their promotion push. A season-long loan was agreed with Karpaty Lviv, and he joined Lechia at the end of June.

  The 2026/27 campaign began disastrously for Lechia, who collected just one point from their first four matches. Shakh missed those games through a minor injury. He came off the bench after just 39 minutes in his first appearance, but was unable to prevent another heavy defeat.

  The following match, however, marked the beginning of a remarkable run. Shakh exploded into form and has not looked back. After his first eight appearances, he had amassed an astonishing seven goals and five assists, quickly becoming one of the breakout stars of the season and helping put Lechia firmly back in the promotion race.

  Transfer outlook

  Poland currently sits 12th in the UEFA league ranking, although the second tier is naturally a significant step below the Ekstraklasa.

  Shakh is on a season-long loan from Karpaty Lviv, where his contract runs until 31 December 2028. According to an investigative report by prominent Ukrainian football outlet TaToTake, Lechia do not have an option to buy him. That changes the situation considerably, and if Shakh maintains this level, I would expect interest from other clubs well before the next summer window. A move could already become a talking point in January.

  Ekstraklasa clubs will naturally be among the first to take a look, but interest from Germany, including the 2. Bundesliga, would not be surprising either. Norwegian clubs would also be wise to monitor the situation and consider entering the race. These leagues would all represent a step up, but Shakh's pace, acceleration, progressive ball carrying and goal threat give him a profile that could translate well to high-tempo football.

  It is difficult to estimate what Karpaty Lviv would demand to let him leave, particularly with his contract running until the end of 2028. Our best estimate would be a fee in the region of €1–2 million. That is a substantial investment for a player currently performing in the Polish second tier, but if his current form is a sign of what he can deliver at a higher level, it could prove to be money well spent.
