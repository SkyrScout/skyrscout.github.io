  Ivan Krasnov | 16-year-old Attacking Midfielder | Estonia

  Ivan Krasnov is a 16-year-old attacking midfielder currently playing for FC Nõmme United in the Estonian Premium Liiga. His official height is listed at 180 cm, although I suspect he may have grown another couple of centimetres during the season. At just 16, he could easily have another growth spurt ahead of him and potentially reach 185 cm or more.

  His biggest strengths are undoubtedly his acceleration and dribbling ability, although he also has good pace over distance. He is particularly effective at driving forward with the ball at speed, combining close control with the ability to change direction and carry the attack into more advanced areas.

  Krasnov began the season in a more traditional No. 8 role before being moved further out to the left wing. From that position, he frequently drifted inside into the left half-space to receive the ball and get involved in the build-up. As the season progressed, he increasingly found himself operating as a No. 10, with the freedom to move between the central areas and both half-spaces. He has also been deployed as a false nine. This versatility is encouraging, but it also highlights his ability to read the game, anticipate spaces and find pockets between opposition lines.

  Although primarily an attack-minded player, Krasnov also contributes well defensively. He has a good instinct for intercepting passes and quickly turning defensive recoveries into attacking transitions, either through progressive carries or intelligent, well-weighted through balls. His passing range is impressive for his age, covering everything from short combinations to longer passes and incisive balls beyond the defensive line.

  His work rate and stamina are very good, and he is willing to contribute to the team's high press. Predominantly right-footed, his left foot is not far behind, making him almost two-footed in his passing game. He can also generate powerful shots from distance with either foot, something he has demonstrated with his goals this season. In addition, he looks to threaten the opposition defence with well-timed movement beyond the defensive line, adding another dimension to his attacking game.

  Areas for improvement

  I am going to highlight quite a few areas for improvement, but before doing so, it is important to remember that Krasnov is only 16 years old and has just completed his first season playing senior football against grown men. Everything that follows must be viewed in that context.

  First and foremost, he needs to build more muscle and physical strength. At present, opponents can sometimes push him off the ball too easily, and he needs to become better equipped to handle the physical demands of senior football. There have already been noticeable improvements in this department during the season, which is encouraging.

  Another important area is his overall decision-making, along with the accuracy and precision of his execution. He is capable of producing brilliant technical actions, only to give the ball away moments later in situations where he should have been able to maintain possession. Learning when to take risks and when to keep things simple will be an important part of his development.

  In the final third, his crossing and finishing in one-on-one situations against the goalkeeper also need work. These are areas where greater composure and consistency could make a significant difference to his attacking output.

  Career trajectory and current form

  Ivan Krasnov spent his formative years at various clubs in the greater Tallinn area before joining the youth setup at FC Nõmme United in January 2025. He made his first-team debut for the newly promoted Premium Liiga club in May 2026 against reigning champions FC Flora Tallinn, approximately one month before his 16th birthday.

  The youngster grabbed the opportunity with both hands, or perhaps we should say both feet. He made 18 appearances during the season, performed surprisingly well and finished with two goals and one assist.

  The assist is admittedly somewhat debatable. It came from an intelligent dummy, allowing the ball to run through his legs and reach a teammate. Whether he actually made the slightest contact with the ball is difficult to determine from the available footage. I have nevertheless chosen to credit him with the assist, as the deliberate action was instrumental in creating the opportunity, regardless of whether he touched the ball or not.

  Transfer outlook

  Estonia currently sits 49th in the UEFA league coefficient rankings, and regular first-team football at Premium Liiga level is exactly the kind of experience a talent like Krasnov needs at this stage of his development.

  If he continues to improve in the areas highlighted above, he will be a very exciting player to follow throughout the 2027 season. I am not entirely sure which position will ultimately suit him best. His acceleration, pace and dribbling ability make him an intriguing option as an inverted left winger, where he can attack defenders one-on-one and drive inside. At the same time, his creativity, vision and passing range could make him equally interesting as an attacking No. 8 or a No. 10.

  Krasnov is under contract with FC Nõmme United until 31 December 2029, but at just 16 years old, there is absolutely no reason for him to rush into a move. I believe he would be better served by staying at Nõmme United, who successfully avoided relegation and will remain in the Premium Liiga next season. Another year of regular senior football, with the opportunity to continue developing in a familiar environment, could be extremely valuable.

  The emergence of young Estonian talents in their domestic leagues has been particularly encouraging, and it will be fascinating to follow Krasnov next season. In particular, I will be interested to see whether he can take similar steps forward to those made by Sander Alamaa and Maksimilian Skvortsov during the current season.

  At 16, Krasnov already has a very interesting foundation to build on. There is still plenty of development ahead of him, but his acceleration, dribbling, creativity and positional versatility make him one of the young Estonian players worth keeping a close eye on.
